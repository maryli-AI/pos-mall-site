About POS Mall

A specialist supplier of used and refurbished point-of-sale hardware. We buy, recondition and redistribute commercial checkout equipment — and nothing else.

POS Mall exists for one reason: to keep good commercial hardware in service. A receipt printer or touchscreen terminal that is no longer wanted at one site is usually still perfectly capable of running a checkout lane elsewhere — provided somebody inspects it properly first.

What we do

  • Source used and ex-lease point-of-sale hardware in trade, pallet and container quantities.
  • Strip, clean, inspect and functionally test every unit before it is listed.
  • State exactly what is included with each unit, so there is nothing to discover on arrival.
  • Hold matched stock across brands so a complete station can be supplied as a working set.
  • Quote trade and volume pricing by enquiry, tailored to the quantity and destination.

What we do not do

So there is no confusion later — these are deliberate limits on our business, not temporary gaps:

  • We do not sell POS software. No licences, no subscriptions, no monthly fees. We supply hardware only.
  • We do not operate a buy-back or trade-in service. We are not a place to sell your old equipment to.
  • We do not take orders through this website. The catalogue is quotation-based: you build an enquiry list, we reply with pricing, stock and freight. Nothing is charged online.

Warranty & returns

  • Refurbished hardware carries a 90-day parts-and-labour warranty as standard, unless a listing states otherwise.
  • All used and refurbished stock carries the same 90-day functional warranty, covering the unit working as described.
  • Extended warranty cover is available on volume orders — ask when requesting your quotation.
  • Cosmetic wear described at the point of sale is not covered by warranty.
  • Consumables (till rolls, ribbons, batteries) are non-returnable once opened.

Warranty claims are handled by replacement where stock allows, or by repair. Report a fault within the warranty period and our team will advise on the fastest route.

How to buy

  • 1. Browse or search. Use the category and brand navigation to narrow the catalogue.
  • 2. Build an enquiry list. Add each model you need. Quantities can be adjusted on the enquiry page.
  • 3. Send it to us. Submit the form with your delivery region and any compatibility requirements.
  • 4. Receive a quotation. We confirm stock, pricing and freight, then arrange payment and dispatch.

Payment & shipping

Payment is by bank transfer or on an approved trade account. Pro-forma invoices are available on request. We ship worldwide — pallet and container quantities are quoted on an individual basis, so please include a delivery postcode or destination port in your enquiry.

Questions

If you need a model that is not listed, send us the part number anyway — our stock moves quickly and we routinely source specific models to order. Get in touch or request a quotation.
